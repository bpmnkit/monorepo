Move the editor's start page and file-tab bar onto the bpmnkit.com design system: flat, square,
hairline-ruled, one accent, mono for every label and datum. The welcome actions and examples
each become a single bordered box divided by hairlines rather than gapped cards; the file-type
badges and tab chips drop their blue/purple/amber/green taxonomy for mono marks in the accent;
the active tab's underline sits on the tab row's own rule; and dialogs and the group dropdown
lose their radii and shadows. The logo lockup at the top of the start page is replaced by the
`bpmnkit` wordmark, since the lockup's second brand colour has no place in a one-accent system.
