# Size your environment — Next steps

Now that you understand the factors that influence sizing:

- **SaaS customers:** [Size your SaaS cluster](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/best-practices/architecture/sizing-saas) to select the right cluster size.
- **Self-Managed admins:** Provision your Kubernetes cluster using these [baseline resource settings](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/best-practices/architecture/sizing-self-managed).
- **Validate sizing:** [Run your own benchmarks](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/best-practices/architecture/sizing-benchmarks) to test your specific workload.

For current secondary storage benchmarks, see [RDBMS benchmark results](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/self-managed/concepts/secondary-storage/rdbms-benchmark-results).
