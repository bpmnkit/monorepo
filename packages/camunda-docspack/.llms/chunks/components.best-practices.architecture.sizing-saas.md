# Size your SaaS cluster

Select the right Camunda 8 SaaS cluster size based on your needs.

Select the right Camunda 8 SaaS cluster size based on your needs. For an overview of the factors that influence sizing, see [Size your environment](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/best-practices/architecture/sizing-your-environment).


## Determine your cluster size

Camunda 8 defines four [cluster sizes](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/concepts/clusters#cluster-size) (1x, 2x, 3x, and 4x) you can select after choosing your [cluster type](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/concepts/clusters#cluster-type).

To do so, follow these steps:

1. Calculate your throughput and storage requirements using the guidance in [Size your environment](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/next/components/best-practices/architecture/sizing-your-environment).
2. Use the [sizing tables](#sizing-tables) to find the cluster size that meets your needs.

**Note**
To increase the cluster size beyond 4x, [reach out to Camunda](https://camunda.com/contact-us/). This requires custom sizing and pricing.
